What I'm Building — Ranked by Impact
#1  ·  Highest impact
Cancellation survey insights are analyzed and emailed automatically — one month of Qualtrics data turned into a team-ready summary before I lift a finger.
What's happening today
Manual process — log into Qualtrics, export cancellation survey responses, interpret the trends, write a summary, and distribute to the team. Inconsistent cadence and significant synthesis time each cycle.
What the automated version looks like
A Claude agent calls the Qualtrics API on a set schedule, pulls the past 30 days of cancellation survey responses, identifies top cancellation reasons and trend shifts, then drafts a structured email ready to send to stakeholders. One review, one click to send.
Why it's #1
Cancellation insight is decision-critical for a subscription car wash business — this directly informs retention strategy. It's end-to-end: API fetch → AI analysis → exec-ready output. Highest signal, clearest workflow, most immediate business impact.
Medium Lift — half a day ~3 hrs reclaimed / week Not started
#2
Survey insights write themselves — SMG data flows directly into a weekly exec brief, no manual pull required.
What's happening today
21 manual visits to api.smg.com in 14 days — logging in, pulling reports, interpreting results, and writing summaries by hand. Estimated 3–5 hours/week of repetitive extraction and wordsmithing.
What the automated version looks like
A scheduled Claude agent hits the SMG API on a Monday schedule, pulls the latest survey wave, generates a structured brief (top findings → supporting data → recommendation), and emails or posts to Slack. You review a finished product, not raw data.
Why it's #1
Directly eliminates the manual survey work called out in CLAUDE.md. Highest hours reclaimed. The output is exec-ready, which matches the audience Jonah already serves.
Quick Win — under 2 hours ~4 hrs reclaimed / week Not started
#3
Competitor and trend intelligence arrives in my inbox every morning — I never open LinkedIn for work research again.
What's happening today
645 LinkedIn visits in 14 days. Even if 30% are legitimate competitive research, that's ~200 manual visits to gather scattered signals with no structured output, no changelog, no anomaly detection.
What the automated version looks like
An agent tracks competitors (car wash chains, relevant operators) via LinkedIn, news APIs, and Google Alerts. Daily or weekly digest: new developments → sentiment shift → recommended action. Delivered to email before 8am.
Why it's #2
This is the core of the #1 goal — autonomous competitor intelligence. Reclaims 3+ hours/week and converts scattered browsing into a structured, scheduled signal.
Medium Lift — half a day ~3 hrs reclaimed / week Not started
#4
SQL queries never get written manually — I describe what I need and Snowflake runs it.
What's happening today
6 Snowflake visits indicate active data querying. Writing and debugging SQL is explicitly listed in CLAUDE.md as something to eliminate. Estimated 2–4 hours/week on query construction, debugging, and iteration.
What the automated version looks like
Claude Code or a Claude API integration accepts plain-English data questions, generates and executes SQL against Snowflake, returns formatted results. Common queries become saved templates. No SQL written by hand.
Why it's #3
Explicitly named in CLAUDE.md as a priority elimination. Quick win effort level means it can ship fast. Immediately frees up analyst capacity for higher-value synthesis work.
Quick Win — under 2 hours ~3 hrs reclaimed / week Not started
#5
Google search is replaced by structured AI answers — research that took 20 minutes takes 2.
What's happening today
464 raw Google searches during work hours with no synthesis pipeline. Search → click → read → synthesize manually. High-friction use of time for an analyst who needs structured signal, not a list of links.
What the automated version looks like
Claude with web search, or a Claude project trained on Quick Quack context, answers internal and market questions directly. Replaces click-depth research with one-shot answers. Saves synthesis time on every research session.
Why it's #4
Habit change, not a build — but high leverage. 464 searches/two weeks means even a 50% reduction would reclaim meaningful weekly time with zero engineering effort.
Quick Win — under 2 hours ~2 hrs reclaimed / week Not started
#6
Exec decks draft themselves from data — wordsmithing is review, not creation.
What's happening today
Eliminating PowerPoint wordsmithing is an explicit goal in CLAUDE.md. Desktop app usage isn't captured in the audit — this is a known blind spot. Time cost is real but unmeasured.
What the automated version looks like
Claude takes structured data output (survey results, Snowflake exports) and generates slide-ready bullets in exec-facing format: headline finding → supporting evidence → recommendation. Output goes into a Google Slides or PowerPoint template. Editing, not writing.
Why it's #5
Named elimination target. Lower rank because it requires the SMG/Snowflake automation (#1, #3) to be in place first — the output of those feeds this one. Natural third step in the stack.
Medium Lift — half a day ~2 hrs reclaimed / week Not started
#7
Recurring coordination with Scot and others happens through templates — no blank-page email writing.
What's happening today
Recurring 1:1 with scot@skepfoundation.org confirmed in calendar. Outlook accessed 47× — active email thread management. Without email data connected, exact pattern unknown, but coordination overhead for recurring relationships is a consistent hidden cost.

Quick Win — under 2 hours ~1 hr reclaimed / week Not started
